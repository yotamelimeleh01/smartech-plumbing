Signs your water heater needs attention

  • No hot water, or it runs out fast
  • Water pooling around the base of the tank
  • Rusty or discolored hot water
  • Popping, rumbling or banging noises
  • Pilot light won't stay lit, or the breaker keeps tripping
  • The tank is 10+ years old
Leaking tank? Turn off the cold water valve on top of the heater and shut off the power or gas, then call us.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you come out?

Same-day service is often available. Call us and we'll give you the earliest time we have.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If the tank is leaking from the body or is more than 10 to 12 years old, replacement is usually the better value. Many other problems, like a bad thermostat, element, igniter or valve, can be repaired. We'll walk you through both options and the price before any work starts.

Do you work on tankless water heaters?

Yes. We service tank and tankless water heaters, both gas and electric.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the problem and the parts needed. You'll get the price up front, before any work starts. No surprises.

What should I do if my water heater is leaking?

Turn off the cold water supply valve on top of the tank and turn off the power (electric) or set the gas valve to "off" or "pilot". Then call us.
